Prices and payment

All prices are in Danish kroner including 25% Danish VAT. Shipping costs DKK 49 per order and is shown in the cart before you pay. You pay through Stripe by card or another of the payment methods shown. The amount is charged when you complete the purchase.

The contract

The contract is made when you have paid. The receipt from Stripe by email is your order confirmation. The contract is concluded in Danish under Danish law.

Delivery

We make the tags for your order and usually send them within 3 working days, as a letter or parcel with PostNord, GLS or dao. We ship to Denmark.

Right of withdrawal

You have 14 days to withdraw, counted from the day you receive the goods. Use the button Withdraw from a purchase, write to contact@stemplerind.dk, or use the form at the bottom of this page.

Send the goods back no later than 14 days after you withdraw. You pay for the return. We refund the full amount, including the original shipping, no later than 14 days after we are told about your withdrawal. We may wait until we have received the goods. If you have used the tags more than needed to see that they work, for example stuck them on, we may deduct the loss in value.

Custom designs are made to your order. The right of withdrawal does not apply to them.

Complaints about faults

You have 2 years to complain about faults. If a tag does not work, write to contact@stemplerind.dk. You get a new tag or your money back.

Disputes

Write to us first at contact@stemplerind.dk. If we cannot agree, you can complain to the Danish Consumer Complaints Board, Nævnenes Hus, Toldboden 2, 8800 Viborg, Denmark, via forbrug.dk. If you live in another EU country, the European Consumer Centre Denmark can help.
