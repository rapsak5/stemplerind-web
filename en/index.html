The tags

Every tag is a switch

The first scan turns it on, the next turns it off. That goes for in and out, sick leave, holidays and time off in lieu.

In and out

1st scan
Clocks you in.
2nd scan
Clocks you out. Lunch or a meeting in town: just scan again.

Sick and well

1st scan
Reports you sick and clocks you out.
2nd scan
Reports you well again. Clocking in also ends the sick leave.

Holiday

1st scan
Your holiday starts.
2nd scan
Your holiday ends, and the days are counted.

Time off in lieu

1st scan
Your time off starts.
2nd scan
Your time off ends, and the hours are taken from your balance.
Project tags

Six colours. You decide what they mean.

Red can be a client, green teaching and blue admin. The first time you scan a coloured tag, the app creates the project. You give it a name under Settings.

  • If you are out, the tag clocks you in on the project.
  • If you are in on another project, you switch project.
  • Scan the same project again, and you are back on general time.

Questions

What people ask

What is on the tag?

Only a web address and the app name. No personal data. All tags of the same kind are identical.

Does the phone need internet?

No. The tag is read with NFC, and everything is stored on the phone.

What if I do not have the app?

The tag opens Google Play so you can get it. Next time it opens the app directly.

What if I forget to clock out?

The app tells you the next day and suggests a time based on your other days. You can also edit the times yourself.

Can I have tags in several places?

Yes. Buy extra In and out tags and put them by the door, on your desk and in the car. They all do the same.

Which languages does the app speak?

English, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian and German. Switch with the flag under Settings. The coloured tags are printed in Danish, the symbol tags have no text, and all of them work in every language.

Does it work on iPhone?

The app comes to Android first. The iPhone version is on its way, and the same tags will work there.

Can my employer see my times?

No. Your data stays on your phone. You decide when to export a period and send it.
