StemplerInd privacy policy

Applies to the StemplerInd app (dk.stemplerind.app), published by Berg-Streaming, CVR 40989102. Last updated November 2026.

In short

StemplerInd collects no data. Everything you record stays on your phone. We have no server, no accounts, no ads and no usage statistics.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your clock-ins and clock-outs with time, source (button, NFC tag, widget) and project, if any.
  • Absence you register yourself, such as holidays and sick days. The app stores only the type and the dates, never reasons or health information.
  • Your settings, such as language, weekly hours, working days, projects and your name, if you enter it.

The data is deleted when you uninstall the app or tap Delete all data under Settings.

Sharing

The app sends nothing anywhere. When you export to Excel, Word, PDF or a backup file, the app creates the file on the phone and opens the phone's share menu. You decide where the file goes.

Permissions

  • NFC: to read StemplerInd tags when you hold the phone to them.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are created on the phone.
  • Internet: required by the app's technology, but the app neither downloads nor sends any data.

NFC tags

The tags contain only a short address, such as https://stemplerind.dk/t/skift. They hold no personal information. If the address is opened in a browser, our website receives an ordinary page request, which is not stored or linked to you.

Website and shop

The website stemplerind.dk uses no cookies and no statistics. The fonts are hosted on our own server. When you order tags, you do so by email. We use your details only to ship and invoice your order.

The contact form

If you write to us through the form on the website, your name, email and message are sent to our email through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co). We only use the information to reply to you and delete the message when the matter is closed.
